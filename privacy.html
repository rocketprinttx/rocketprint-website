Privacy Policy

Effective October 28, 2026

Your privacy matters to Rocket Print. This page explains the information we collect when you browse our website, place an order, or send a custom printing request, as well as the options available to you.

1. Information we collect

When you place an order, we may collect your name, email address, telephone number, delivery address, items ordered, sizes, quantities, fulfillment choice, and order notes. If you request custom printing, we may also collect your organization, project description, artwork or reference links, garment preferences, quantities, and requested dates. Information you voluntarily send us through other communications may also be retained to respond to your requests.

Our website uses local browser storage to keep items in your shopping cart and remember your analytics preference. We may also receive basic technical information necessary to protect and operate our website.

2. How we use information

  • Provide price quotes, process orders, coordinate shipping or local fulfillment, and communicate about your purchase.
  • Review custom artwork requests, prepare proofs, and coordinate production or reorders.
  • Maintain business, order, tax, dispute, and security records where necessary.
  • Understand website usage and improve the shopping experience using analytics, subject to the controls explained below.

3. Payments and service providers

Secure checkout is provided by Stripe. Payment card entry occurs in Stripe's embedded checkout; Rocket Print does not use this website to store full payment card numbers. We use Cloudflare for website delivery, security and payment-related services; Google services and Google Apps Script/Sheets for order processing and records; and Microsoft services for business email notifications. These providers may process information necessary to provide those functions under their respective terms and privacy practices.

4. Website analytics, recordings and cookies

Rocket Print uses Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity to measure traffic, product interest, add-to-cart behavior, form interactions, and usability. Microsoft Clarity may record interactions and produce heatmaps; we configure masking for customer-information sections, including checkout and custom request forms. We do not intentionally send names, email addresses, addresses, telephone numbers, custom messages, artwork, or payment details to analytics services.

Analytics cookies are disabled unless you choose to allow them. When cookies are not allowed, analytics providers may still receive limited cookie-free measurements, such as anonymous interaction events or page-view signals. These limited measurements may not support identifying returning visitors or connecting activity across visits. Advertising-related consent stays denied. Necessary technologies, such as cart storage and security checks, are separate from optional analytics cookies.

Your analytics preferences

We don't display an automatic cookie prompt. You can choose whether to allow analytics cookies below, and you can change that choice later from this page. Your selection is saved in this browser.

Analytics cookies: not allowed

5. Security and retention

We use measures intended to protect our website and order processing, including encrypted connections, anti-bot verification and payment checks. No online system can guarantee absolute security. We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill orders, support customers, maintain required records, and meet applicable legal obligations. Retention periods can vary by record type.

6. Sharing and your choices

We share information with service providers as needed to fulfill orders, run the website, and provide business services. We do not sell customer contact lists as a business practice. You can ask about the information Rocket Print holds about you or request correction or deletion, subject to applicable obligations and lawful exceptions.

7. Children

Our online store is intended for adults purchasing apparel. We do not intentionally seek to collect personal information directly from children under 13.

8. Other websites

Links to external services, including Stripe and social media, may have independent privacy policies. Their practices are governed by those services.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our website, services or applicable requirements change. The effective date above will identify the version shown here.

10. Contact Rocket Print

For privacy questions or requests, email shoprocketprint@gmail.com. Rocket Print is based in Wellington, Texas.
