About Rocket Print

Rooted in Wellington. Made to stand out.

Rocket Print is a Wellington, Texas apparel and custom printing company built for the people, schools, teams, businesses, and events that make small towns feel like home. We focus on clean, wearable design and quality T-shirts for local collections and custom projects.

Our goal is simple: make local apparel feel premium, timeless, and worth wearing long after the event, season, or game is over. Small town. Big pride.

FAQ

Good to know.

A few helpful details about ordering, garment care, artwork, and custom projects.

How long does an order usually take?

Most orders are ready in about two weeks. Turnaround can take longer depending on the garment, quantity, availability, and whether the artwork is already print-ready. We confirm the expected timeline before your order is finalized.

How should I wash printed apparel?

For the best life of the print, wash garments inside out in cold water with mild detergent. Avoid bleach and fabric softener, and tumble dry low or hang dry when possible. Do not iron directly over the printed design.

Do you have a minimum order?

No minimum is required for most standard Rocket Print apparel. Larger group or organization orders can also be quoted together.

Do you offer discounts for larger orders?

Yes. Schools, teams, businesses, events, and organizations ordering multiple pieces can request quantity pricing based on the garment, print, and total order size.

What kind of artwork should I send?

High-resolution PNG files with transparent backgrounds are ideal. Vector files such as SVG, EPS, or PDF are also excellent when available. If your artwork needs cleanup, we can review it before printing.

Can you recreate or clean up a logo?

Yes. We can help clean up low-resolution artwork, rebuild simple logos, or prepare an existing design for apparel printing. More involved design work may be quoted separately.

Can I see the design before it is printed?

Yes. Custom orders receive a digital proof for approval before production begins so you can confirm the artwork, placement, and overall look.

Can custom items be returned or exchanged?

Because custom apparel is made specifically for the customer, approved custom orders are generally final sale. If there is a production defect or we made an error, contact us and we will make it right.

Can you print copyrighted or trademarked artwork?

Customers must have permission to use any logos, characters, trademarks, or other protected artwork they provide. We may ask for confirmation of permission before producing an order.

Do you keep custom designs on file for reorders?

Yes. Once we complete a custom project, we can retain the approved design information to make future reorders easier.
