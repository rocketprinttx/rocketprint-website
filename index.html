About Rocket Print

Rooted in Wellington. Made to stand out.

Rocket Print is a Wellington, Texas apparel and custom printing company built for the people, schools, teams, businesses, and events that make small towns feel like home. We focus on clean, wearable design and quality apparel — from tees and hoodies to polos, crewnecks, and custom pieces.

Our goal is simple: make local apparel feel premium, timeless, and worth wearing long after the event, season, or game is over. Small town. Big pride.

FAQ

Good to know.

Can I order online yet?

Our online shop is coming soon. Once it launches, you’ll be able to browse available designs, garment options, sizes, and colors directly online.

What does Rocket Print print on?

We print on t-shirts, hoodies, sweatshirts, polo shirts, long-sleeve shirts, crewnecks, and other apparel based on the needs of the project.

Do you offer custom apparel?

Yes. We create custom apparel for schools, teams, businesses, clubs, events, reunions, organizations, and personal projects.

Can you do business or organization apparel?

Yes. We produce branded apparel for businesses, employees, crews, teams, clubs, events, promotions, and community organizations.

Do you create the design too?

Yes. You can bring us a finished logo or design, or start with an idea, sketch, reference, or concept and we can help turn it into a print-ready design.

Why is downtown Wellington featured on the site?

Wellington is home. The downtown image is part of the Rocket Print story and represents the small-town pride behind the brand. It is separate from the artwork used on our apparel.

Do you add new designs regularly?

Yes. We add Wellington releases, school-spirit designs, local collections, seasonal ideas, and custom work as new designs are completed.

Can I order for a group or event?

Yes. We work with group orders for teams, businesses, schools, reunions, fundraisers, events, clubs, and other organizations.
